## Cross-cutting dimensions

Every content item declares independently:

- conceptual and implementation difficulty (1–5)
- scale and ambiguity (1–5)
- expected seniority
- interview duration
- prerequisite depth
- role families and secondary skills
- learning objectives and prerequisite question references
- company-style relevance with a public-theme rationale

## Role families

Backend engineer, data engineer, analytics engineer, data architect, ML engineer, research engineer, AI infrastructure engineer, applied AI engineer, security engineer, engineering manager, staff engineer, principal engineer, and technical lead.

## Skill identifiers

Skills use stable lowercase kebab-case identifiers. They are grouped under algorithms, language engineering, data systems, distributed computing, product architecture, ML lifecycle, generative AI, AI infrastructure, security/privacy, operations, and leadership. Renames require aliases rather than destructive identifier changes.

## Originality policy

Each question records authoring method, source class, source notes, reviewer identity, and a content hash. Public material can inform competencies and constraints, but prompts and solutions must be independently written. No field may claim a question appeared in an employer interview.
