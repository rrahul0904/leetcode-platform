# Product Requirements Document

## Product promise

Rigor prepares experienced engineers to practice the implementation, architecture, operational, and leadership judgment expected in senior through principal interviews. Feedback is evidence-based and transparent. Rigor does not guarantee compensation, interviews, offers, or employment.

## Primary users

- Senior engineer deepening systems breadth
- Staff candidate practicing ambiguous architecture and influence
- Principal candidate demonstrating strategy, governance, and migration judgment
- Data/ML/AI specialist expanding production-system fluency
- Content author and independent reviewer maintaining the bank
- Organization administrator operating a private program

## Initial outcomes

Users can set target roles, browse and filter honestly labeled content, inspect prerequisites, work through an exercise, submit evidence, and understand their next practice recommendation. Authors can version a structured question and move it through explicit review states.

## Functional scope by phase

### Foundation and first vertical slice

Taxonomy, complete manifest, canonical schemas, content validation, onboarding, searchable catalog, structured question detail, submission contract, admin authoring contract, and progress dashboard.

### Execution

Isolated Python and PostgreSQL sandboxes, hidden tests, deterministic scoring, execution telemetry, quotas, and cleanup evidence.

### Design and intelligence

Modeling/architecture canvases, design documents, stateful AI interviews, evidence-linked evaluation, personalized learning, and spaced repetition.

### Full content release

At least 1,000 validated and published questions; 1,350 remains the initial target.

## Non-functional requirements

- WCAG 2.2 AA target and keyboard-first workflows
- strict typing and versioned contracts
- tenant-aware authorization and immutable administrative audit records
- no candidate code in the application plane
- graceful loading, empty, partial failure, error, autosave, and recovery states
- measurable SLOs before production launch
- explicit consent before external AI processing of private material

## Success measures

Published/validated content ratio, completion consistency, reduced hint dependence, retention, rubric improvement, execution reliability, search usefulness, accessibility defects, and incident/security metrics. Readiness is an estimate with confidence, never an employment guarantee.
