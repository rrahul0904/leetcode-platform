## Evidence policy

`OBSERVED` means directly present in a named source. `INFERRED` means a reasoned conclusion with its supporting observations stated. `UNKNOWN` means the necessary evidence is unavailable. `CONTRADICTION` means authoritative sources disagree. `VERIFIED` is reserved for an executed check with a retained receipt that proves the stated behavior. CI success and deployment status are not substitutes for runtime verification.

No donor code, hidden APIs, question banks, branding, or assets are copied. This ledger records evidence provenance, not donor authorization.
