# ADR-005: Content Provenance and Publication

- Status: Accepted
- Date: 2026-10-13

## Decision

Only original or legitimately licensed content with recorded provenance may be published. Publication requires the repository's technical/editorial/rights gates, validated reference behavior and public/hidden fixture separation. Donor names or product descriptions are not evidence of permission or behavior.

## Consequences

Planning manifests, source registries, test fixtures and historical counts are not published inventory. Current production counts must come from a database reconciliation. LeetCode/HackerRank automatic collection remains prohibited. Unknown donor-specific behavior is omitted until evidence is public or authorized and a clean-room packet is ready.
