Frequently asked questions

What the demo does—and what it does not pretend to do.

Is the demo using the million-row bank directly?⌄

It uses the uploaded 1M-v1 manifest and generator families for subject coverage and representative interactions. The full Parquet corpus stays a governed import source rather than being bundled into the browser.

Are all one million rows independently authored concepts?⌄

No. The source manifest explicitly says exact text and fingerprints are unique while concept families are parameterized across workload, industry, constraints, scale, latency, retention, source count, region count and objectives.

What is actually interactive?⌄

The globe rotates and supports drag/touch, subject coverage is selectable, questions are searchable/filterable, reasoning can be revealed, the architecture answer box produces a deterministic rubric, the theme switches and the FAQ expands inline.

Does this demo fake AI or code execution?⌄

No. The public preview does not claim a model call or isolated runner when one is not configured. The rubric is deterministic and labeled as such.

Can the 100K and 1M sources both be kept?⌄

Yes. The 100K bank can remain the smaller validated slice and the 1M bank the broader expansion set, each registered with separate provenance, checksums and import jobs.
